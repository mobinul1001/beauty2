Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Eve Beauty Source collects through this website and how it is used. Eve Beauty Source operates beauty supply stores in Theater District (Manhattan), Flushing and Jamaica (Queens), New York.

Information you give us

This website does not have forms that collect your name, email address or other personal details. If you call or visit a store, or contact us in some other way, we use what you share only to help you. We do not sell this information.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of the visit. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and product photos from Amazon and similar image hosts. Your browser connects to those services when it loads a page, so they can see your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies.

Links to Amazon and other sites

Product buttons on this site open Amazon. Some are affiliate links, described in our affiliate disclosure. When you click one, Amazon and its partners may use cookies to track the referral and handle your purchase. Purchases, payment details and orders are handled by Amazon, not by us. Please read Amazon's privacy notice before you buy. The same applies to our links to Instagram, Facebook, Yelp and other sites.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set its own advertising cookies. If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy to say so.

Your choices

If you have given us personal information and want to see, correct or delete it, contact us using the details below. You can also block cookies in your browser settings.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact page or call +1 718-353-9993.
