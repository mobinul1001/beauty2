About Eve Beauty Source

A beauty supply store in Downtown Flushing, Queens.

The store

Eve Beauty Source is a beauty supply store with three locations in New York City: Theater District (778 8th Ave), Flushing (136-14 Roosevelt Ave) and Jamaica (220-06 Hempstead Ave). Shoppers rate the Flushing store 4.3 out of 5 across 427 Google reviews.

The shelves cover hair care, skincare, makeup, body care, nails, beauty tools, bath and wellness products, and everyday accessories.
